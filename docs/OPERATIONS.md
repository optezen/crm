# Business Operations: Appointments, Inventory, Billing

Migrations `0010` (config + appointments), `0011` (inventory), `0012` (billing), `0013` (automation + AI integration). Nothing is industry specific and nothing is pre-seeded: owners define appointment types, staff hours, products, locations, suppliers and taxes.

## How it integrates

* **Builder / configuration.** Enablement and rules live in the versioned workspace configuration under `operations` (`appointments`, `inventory`, `billing`), validated in TypeScript (`validateOperations`) and in SQL (`ops_validate`, wired in by renaming the 0006 validator to `validate_workspace_definition_core`). Builder -> *Business operations* edits the same draft as everything else, so review/diff, publish, version history and rollback all apply. **Disabling a module never deletes data**: the module's screens are hidden, new writes are refused (`ops_require`), reads keep working; re-enabling restores it. Custom fields of appointments and products reuse the custom-module field model (`record_validate`); once published they can be hidden but not removed or retyped.
* **CRM.** Appointments and invoices link to `contacts` / `leads` with composite foreign keys (an id from another organization cannot be referenced). Writes are `SECURITY DEFINER` functions that derive the actor from `auth.uid()`, check membership, role and the live configuration, then write the rows, an audit row and a domain event in one transaction. Organization ids and money totals are never accepted from the browser.
* **Automation Engine.** Events: `appointment.created|rescheduled|cancelled|completed`, `inventory.low_stock|stock_received|stock_adjusted|stock_consumed|stock_transferred`, `invoice.created|issued|paid`, `payment.received|refunded`, `credit_note.issued`. The former contract-only triggers (`appointment.created`, `payment.received`) are now real. An automation on these can only be **published while the matching module is enabled**. Conditions/templates use `appointment.*`, `inventory.*`, `invoice.*`, `payment.*`; actions are the generic ones (notify the event actor, wait, webhook request). Lead/task actions remain limited to lead/task triggers.
* **AI assistant.** Read-only, authorized tools: `get_operations_status`, `list_appointments`, `get_inventory_status`, `list_stock_movements` (everyone), `list_invoices`, `get_billing_summary` (owners, admins, managers). Enabling/disabling a module is a proposal (`propose_module_changes`) that a human approves and publishes. The assistant never says a payment was processed externally.
* **Integration Hub.** Payments are recorded manually. A future payment-provider adapter must post through the same internal function (`billing_post_payment`), via `billing_apply_provider_payment` (service role only; needs a *connected* `payments` connection of the same organization; idempotent per provider reference). It is inert today because the `payments` provider cannot be connected yet.

## Appointments

Types with durations; weekly staff hours (minutes since local midnight, per organization time zone) and time off; statuses `scheduled -> confirmed -> completed`, `cancelled`, `no_show`. **Overlap is enforced by a PostgreSQL exclusion constraint** (`appointments_no_staff_overlap`, needs `btree_gist`) on `(organization, staff, time range)` for live appointments, so concurrent requests cannot double-book; the function adds friendly checks (working hours when enforced, time off). Permissions: any member books; creator, assigned staff or managers change/cancel; only the assigned staff or managers complete; managers+ manage types, hours and time off. `appointment_free_slots` powers the booking screen. **No reminders are sent** (no messaging provider is connected); use an automation `request_integration` only once a provider exists.

## Inventory

`stock_levels` change only inside `inv_apply()`: row lock `FOR UPDATE`, update, append to the immutable `stock_movements` ledger, in one transaction; transfers and first-expiry-first-out consumption lock rows in a fixed order (no deadlocks). A trigger refuses negative quantities unless `allow_negative_stock` is on (never for batch-tracked products). Receipts carry supplier and purchase reference; lots and expiry are optional per configuration; expired lots are blocked from use by default. Adjustments set the *counted* quantity (difference computed under the lock) and need a reason. `inventory_reconcile` proves levels equal the ledger. Low-stock events fire when a product's total crosses down through its threshold. Members read and consume; managers+ receive, adjust, transfer and manage master data.

## Billing

Drafts and issued invoices; lines with percent/amount discounts and configurable exclusive taxes; any 3-letter currency (2 decimals assumed); exact `numeric` arithmetic computed by `billing_calc` (each line's gross, discount and tax rounded to 2 decimals, half away from zero; totals are sums of rounded lines). Issuing allocates a gap-free number, freezes a snapshot (lines, tax rates, bill-to, totals) and makes the invoice, its lines and the snapshot immutable. `balance_due = SUM(invoice_ledger)`: issued `+total`, payment `-amount`, credit note `-amount`, refund `+amount`. Payments cannot exceed the outstanding amount and accept an idempotency key; credit notes (owner/admin) reverse tax proportionally and cannot exceed the invoice; refunds (owner/admin) only return what the customer is owed after credit notes and never more than was received. `billing_reconcile` checks ledger vs documents. Financial tables are readable by owners, admins and managers only.

## Operational notes and limits

Apply 0010-0013 after 0009 (the `btree_gist` extension is created by 0010; on Supabase it is available). Timestamps in forms use the browser's time zone for time-off entries; booking slots use the organization time zone. Not built: appointment reminders, recurring appointments, per-location user permissions, costing / valuation, purchase orders as documents, invoice PDFs/emails, tax-inclusive pricing, multi-currency conversion, void-after-issue (use a credit note), editing custom *product* fields in the product form, payment gateways. PGlite tests cannot run parallel connections; `tests/integration/operations.itest.mjs` does (live, not run here).
